Privacy Policy for SquadVault

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in to SquadVault, we collect only the information needed to run the app:

  • Account Information: Your email address and authentication identifiers provided via Email/Password or GitHub OAuth.
  • Profile Data: The custom username you choose to display to other squad members.
  • User Content: Squad names, invite codes, and messages or links you voluntarily post inside your squad vaults.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your session and keep your account secure.
  • Allow you to create, join, and participate in private squad vaults.
  • Maintain moderation logs and enforce community guidelines.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

SquadVault uses Supabase for secure database storage and user authentication, and GitHub OAuth for optional third-party login. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your personal information on SquadVault, including the right to access, update, or delete specific pieces of your data:

  • Vault Posts: You can delete any individual message or link you have posted at any time directly inside the SquadVault feed.
  • Profile & Username: You have the right to change or clear your display username and profile details.
  • Email & Account Info: You can request the removal or update of your associated email address and authentication credentials, or request full deletion of your entire account and all linked data.

To request specific data removal (such as clearing your email or wiping your entire profile), contact the site administrator and your records will be permanently deleted from our database.
